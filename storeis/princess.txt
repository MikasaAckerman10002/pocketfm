Episode 1: The Girl No One Could Identify

It was 8:45 AM on a rainy Monday in New York City.

Traffic crawled through Manhattan as thousands of people hurried toward work. Horns echoed between skyscrapers while news channels reported another cyberattack on one of the country's largest banks.

Inside a crowded subway station, twenty-three-year-old **Emma Carter** adjusted her backpack and checked her phone.

Battery: 7%.

No messages.

No missed calls.

Just another ordinary day.

Emma worked as a graphic designer for a small startup. She rented a tiny apartment, survived on instant noodles, and spent weekends volunteering at an animal shelter.

She had no family.

At least...

That's what she believed.

---

As Emma exited the station, she noticed something strange.

Across the street stood three black SUVs.

Their windows were tinted.

The engines were running.

Six men in black suits stepped out simultaneously.

Every one of them looked directly at her.

Emma frowned.

"Do I know them?"

One of the men spoke into an earpiece.

"Target confirmed."

Before Emma could react, they started walking toward her.

Fast.

Too fast.

Fear shot through her body.

She turned around and ran.

---

People stared as she pushed through the crowded sidewalks.

She didn't understand why anyone would chase her.

She wasn't rich.

She wasn't famous.

She had never committed a crime.

Then why?

She ducked into a narrow alley.

The footsteps grew louder.

One man appeared at the entrance.

Another blocked the exit.

Emma was trapped.

The tallest man slowly removed his sunglasses.

"Miss Emma Carter."

She stayed silent.

"We're here to protect you."

Emma laughed nervously.

"That's the oldest lie in the world."

Another black SUV suddenly crashed through a nearby fence.

Gunshots erupted.

The suited men immediately drew pistols.

"GET DOWN!"

Emma dropped to the ground as bullets shattered windows.

Someone was trying to kill her.

---

Within seconds, another group appeared.

This time...

They wore tactical gear with no insignias.

No names.

No badges.

Only black masks.

Their leader pointed directly at Emma.

"Take her alive."

The alley exploded into chaos.

Bullets ricocheted off concrete.

Car alarms screamed.

People fled in every direction.

Just when Emma thought she was about to die...

A black motorcycle burst through the smoke.

The rider grabbed her arm.

"Hold on!"

"What? Who are you?"

"No time."

The motorcycle shot through the alley, weaving between abandoned cars before disappearing into city traffic.

---

Ten minutes later...

They stopped inside an underground parking garage.

The rider removed his helmet.

He looked about thirty.

Sharp blue eyes.

Short dark hair.

Military posture.

"My name is Daniel."

Emma stepped backward.

"Who are you?"

"I'm the reason you're still alive."

"I don't even know you!"

Daniel took out a tablet.

He opened a classified file.

Emma's photograph appeared on the screen.

Underneath it...

A single line.

**REAL NAME: PRINCESS ELENA ASTOR**

Emma stared at him.

"...That's impossible."

Daniel looked her straight in the eyes.

"Everything you've known about your life is a lie."

---

Hundreds of miles away...

Inside the headquarters of one of the world's largest technology companies...

An elderly billionaire watched security footage of Emma escaping.

He smiled.

"So..."

"We've finally found her."

He pressed a red button.

The conference room doors locked automatically.

Every executive looked nervous.

"The princess is alive."

Silence filled the room.

Then he gave a single order.

"Bring her to me."

"No matter the cost."

---

That night...

Emma returned to her apartment.

The door was open.

Someone had searched every room.

Nothing was stolen.

Except...

A photograph.

The only picture she had of herself as a child.

On the empty table lay a single black envelope.

Inside was a handwritten note.

Episode 2: The Truth Hidden in Plain Sight

The rain continued long after midnight.

Emma sat alone in her apartment, staring at the black envelope lying on her coffee table. The handwritten message echoed in her mind.

"If you want to know who you really are... don't trust anyone."

She looked around the room.

Every drawer had been opened.

Her wardrobe was a mess.

Books lay scattered across the floor.

Whoever had searched her apartment knew exactly what they were looking for.

But why take only an old childhood photograph?

Emma picked up her phone.

No signal.

She frowned.

Her Wi-Fi had stopped working.

A notification suddenly appeared on the screen.

UNKNOWN DEVICE CONNECTED TO YOUR ACCOUNT

Before she could tap it...

The phone shut down.

A knock came at the door.

Three slow knocks.

Then silence.

Emma froze.

She quietly picked up a kitchen knife and walked toward the entrance.

Another knock.

This time louder.

"Emma..."

It was Daniel's voice.

She hesitated before unlocking the door.

Daniel rushed inside and immediately closed the curtains.

"We have to leave."

"What happened?"

"They found this place."

"You said I could trust you."

"I said I was trying to keep you alive."

He pulled out a small device from his jacket.

A red light blinked continuously.

"They planted surveillance equipment somewhere in your apartment."

Emma's eyes widened.

"In here?"

Daniel nodded.

"They've probably been listening to every conversation for hours."

He searched the room carefully.

After several minutes, he reached the smoke detector on the ceiling.

Click.

Inside was a tiny black microphone no larger than a fingernail.

Emma couldn't believe it.

"They've been watching me..."

"For much longer than you think."

Minutes later, they left through the building's emergency staircase instead of using the elevator.

Outside, Daniel drove an ordinary delivery van instead of the motorcycle.

"No trackers?"

Emma asked.

Daniel smiled.

"This vehicle officially belongs to a bakery."

Emma almost laughed.

"You steal bread trucks now?"

"I borrow them."

They drove for nearly an hour before entering an abandoned warehouse near the city's old shipping docks.

Inside...

Nothing looked abandoned.

Computer servers filled the room.

Large digital maps covered entire walls.

Dozens of people worked quietly in front of holographic displays.

Some monitored satellites.

Others intercepted encrypted communications.

Emma looked around in disbelief.

"What is this place?"

Daniel answered calmly.

"It's called The Haven."

"Our safe house."

A woman in her forties approached them.

She wore jeans, a black jacket, and glasses.

Unlike everyone else, she looked completely relaxed.

"So..."

She smiled.

"This is our missing princess."

Emma immediately interrupted.

"Stop calling me that."

"I've never been a princess."

The woman extended her hand.

"My name is Maya."

"I used to work for Interpol."

"What happened?"

"I discovered things I wasn't supposed to."

Maya led Emma into a conference room.

A massive digital screen lit up.

Emma's childhood picture appeared.

Beside it...

Another photograph.

This one showed a luxurious mansion in Switzerland.

Standing outside were an elegant couple holding a newborn baby.

Emma slowly stood up.

The baby wore the exact same silver necklace she still had around her neck.

Maya zoomed in.

"This picture was taken twenty-three years ago."

Emma whispered,

"Who are they?"

Daniel answered.

"Your parents."

Silence filled the room.

Emma couldn't breathe.

Maya continued.

"Official records say your parents died in a private jet accident."

"But..."

Daniel changed the image.

The airplane wreckage disappeared.

Satellite footage replaced it.

"There was never an accident."

"The explosion happened after the aircraft landed."

Emma stared at the screen.

"You mean..."

"They were assassinated."

Maya began explaining everything.

Emma wasn't the princess of a country.

She was the last surviving heir of the Astor Consortium.

A private international organization founded nearly two hundred years ago.

It controlled humanitarian projects...

Medical research...

Global infrastructure...

Billions of dollars in investments...

And confidential technologies decades ahead of the public.

Unlike ordinary corporations...

Leadership wasn't elected.

It was inherited.

The legal successor possessed voting authority over every board member.

That successor...

Was Emma.

Emma shook her head.

"This sounds insane."

"I work in graphic design."

"I struggle to pay rent."

Daniel smiled.

"Exactly."

"That's why they never found you."

Maya projected another file.

The names of twelve board members appeared.

Three names were highlighted in red.

"They orchestrated your parents' deaths."

"They've controlled the Consortium ever since."

Emma frowned.

"So why chase me now?"

"Because someone leaked classified records proving you're alive."

"And if shareholders discover the legitimate heir survived..."

"The current leadership loses everything."

Suddenly...

Every monitor inside the warehouse turned black.

Technicians looked confused.

"What happened?"

One engineer shouted,

"We've lost satellite feeds!"

Another yelled,

"Our firewall's collapsing!"

Then...

Words slowly appeared on every screen.

HELLO, EMMA.

The room fell silent.

Another line appeared.

WELCOME BACK.

Emma felt a chill.

"Who is doing this?"

Nobody answered.

The text changed again.

YOUR FATHER TRUSTED THE WRONG PEOPLE.

DON'T MAKE THE SAME MISTAKE.

Daniel immediately disconnected the central server.

The message disappeared.

Power returned.

But everyone looked terrified.

Maya whispered,

"They found us..."

A young programmer rushed into the room.

"We traced the intrusion."

"Where?"

He swallowed nervously.

"It didn't come from outside."

Everyone looked at each other.

"What do you mean?"

"The hacker is already inside our network."

Emergency alarms echoed through the warehouse.

Security doors slammed shut.

Automatic lockdown initiated.

Red lights flashed everywhere.

A voice announced over the speakers.

Security breach detected.

Unknown personnel inside the facility.

Daniel drew his pistol.

"Nobody moves."

Emma looked around.

The people she'd met only minutes earlier suddenly became strangers.

Was one of them secretly working for the people hunting her?

The lights suddenly went out.

Complete darkness.

Only emergency lights illuminated the corridors.

Footsteps echoed somewhere nearby.

Slow.

Calm.

Confident.

Someone was walking toward them.

Emma held her breath.

A figure emerged from the darkness.

A young man in an expensive black suit.

No weapon.

No fear.

He smiled as if he had been invited.

"My apologies for the dramatic entrance."

Daniel immediately aimed his gun.

"Identify yourself."

The man ignored him.

Instead...

He looked directly at Emma.

"So..."

"We finally meet."

Emma frowned.

"Who are you?"

The stranger gave a polite bow.

"My name is Adrian Volkov."

"I'm the man your parents trusted more than anyone."

He paused.

"And unfortunately..."

"I'm also the man responsible for their deaths."

The room fell completely silent.

Daniel's finger tightened on the trigger.

Emma stared at Adrian, unable to speak.

His expression showed no guilt.

Only regret.

Then he quietly added,

"But if you shoot me now..."

"You'll never learn who actually ordered the assassination."
Episode 3: The Princess Who Chose Her Own Destiny**



The warehouse fell into complete silence.

Every weapon in the room was pointed at **Adrian Volkov**.

Daniel's finger rested on the trigger.

"One more word," Daniel said coldly, "and I'll end this."

Adrian didn't move.

He slowly raised both hands.

"If killing me solved everything..."

"I would've done it myself years ago."

Emma stepped forward.

"Tell me the truth."

Adrian looked into her eyes.

"For twenty-three years..."

"I've waited for this moment."

---

Daniel shouted,

"Don't listen to him!"

But Emma raised her hand.

"Five minutes."

"If he's lying..."

"You can arrest him."

Daniel reluctantly lowered his weapon.

---

Adrian walked to the digital screen.

He inserted a small encrypted drive into the computer.

Immediately...

Old surveillance footage appeared.

Date:

**April 18, 2003**

The footage showed Emma's parents entering a private conference room inside the Astor Consortium headquarters.

Several executives followed.

Among them...

Three faces Emma had already seen on Maya's investigation board.

Victor Graves.

Olivia Monroe.

Richard Hale.

Adrian paused the video.

"They wanted control."

"Your parents refused."

Emma whispered,

"So they killed them?"

Adrian nodded.

"But not immediately."

---

The next recording began.

Emma's father stood beside Adrian inside a private office.

"If anything happens to us..."

Her father handed Adrian a silver pendant.

"You protect Elena."

Adrian accepted it.

"I'll give my life."

Emma instinctively touched the silver necklace she had worn since childhood.

It was identical.

---

Another recording started.

This one had no audio.

Only security cameras.

Emma watched in horror.

Masked gunmen stormed the building.

Explosions shook the corridors.

Employees ran in every direction.

Her father fought back.

Her mother carried baby Emma toward an emergency elevator.

The video suddenly froze.

Adrian looked away.

"I failed."

"I reached them..."

"Thirty seconds too late."

---

Emma could barely breathe.

Tears rolled down her face.

"They really loved me..."

Adrian quietly nodded.

"More than anything."

---

Suddenly...

Every screen inside the warehouse turned red.

A new message appeared.

**LIVE TRANSMISSION DETECTED**

A face slowly emerged.

Victor Graves.

Chairman of the Astor Consortium.

The man believed to have died five years earlier.

He smiled.

"Hello, Emma."

"You've grown."

Emma stared at the screen.

"So you're alive."

Victor laughed.

"Obviously."

"I've spent twenty-three years protecting what's mine."

"You murdered my parents."

"I built an empire."

"They stood in my way."

---

Emma clenched her fists.

"What do you want?"

Victor leaned closer to the camera.

"You."

"The Consortium legally belongs to you."

"As long as you're alive..."

"My position is temporary."

He smiled again.

"So..."

"I've decided to remove the problem."

---

At that exact moment...

The warehouse exploded.

Glass shattered.

Smoke filled the room.

Masked mercenaries stormed inside from every entrance.

Automatic gunfire echoed through the building.

Daniel tackled Emma behind a concrete pillar.

"Stay down!"

Maya shouted,

"Everyone to defensive positions!"

Chaos erupted.

---

Daniel and his team fought desperately.

Mercenaries advanced floor by floor.

Drones flew through broken windows.

Flash grenades illuminated the warehouse.

Emma watched innocent people risking their lives to protect her.

She realized something.

She couldn't keep running forever.

---

She stood up.

Daniel grabbed her arm.

"What are you doing?"

Emma looked at him calmly.

"If I keep hiding..."

"People will keep dying because of me."

Daniel shook his head.

"There has to be another way."

Emma smiled sadly.

"No."

"There has to be an end."

---

She walked directly into the center of the warehouse.

Every mercenary stopped.

Every gun turned toward her.

Emma spoke loudly.

"I'm Emma Carter."

"And yes..."

"I'm Elena Astor."

"If you want me..."

"I'm right here."

---

A helicopter landed outside.

Victor Graves stepped out himself.

For the first time in twenty-three years...

The two finally met.

Victor slowly applauded.

"Your father had the same courage."

Emma stared at him.

"And the same belief."

Victor laughed.

"Belief doesn't win."

"Power does."

---

Emma reached into her pocket.

Victor smiled.

"Looking for a weapon?"

Instead...

Emma removed a small flash drive.

"I came prepared."

Victor's smile disappeared.

"What is that?"

Adrian answered.

"Every document."

"Every illegal transfer."

"Every assassination order."

"Every offshore account."

"Every recording."

"We uploaded everything."

Victor looked confused.

"Uploaded?"

Emma smiled.

"To every major news network."

"To international law enforcement."

"To financial regulators."

"And..."

She held up her phone.

"It started streaming live..."

"Five minutes ago."

---

Victor's face turned pale.

He grabbed his phone.

Breaking News notifications flooded the screen.

**GLOBAL CORPORATE CONSPIRACY EXPOSED**

**ASTOR CONSORTIUM CHAIRMAN LINKED TO MULTIPLE ASSASSINATIONS**

**INTERNATIONAL ARREST WARRANT ISSUED**

His empire collapsed...

In seconds.

---

Police sirens echoed outside.

Then military helicopters.

FBI vehicles.

Interpol agents.

International task forces.

The warehouse was surrounded.

Victor slowly looked around.

There was nowhere left to run.

He laughed quietly.

"You really are your father's daughter."

He dropped his weapon.

Agents rushed forward and arrested him.

The remaining mercenaries surrendered.

It was over.

---

Three months later...

Emma stood in front of thousands of employees at Astor Consortium Headquarters.

Reporters from around the world filled the auditorium.

A journalist asked,

"Miss Astor..."

"Will you become the new Chairwoman?"

Emma smiled.

"No."

The room became silent.

"My parents built this organization to help people."

"Not to create another dynasty."

"So today..."

"We're changing everything."

She announced that ownership of the Consortium would become a transparent public trust governed by an independent international board, with strict oversight and no hereditary succession.

The audience erupted in applause.

---

Later that evening...

Emma returned to the small apartment where she had once lived.

It hadn't changed much.

The same coffee mug.

The same old couch.

The same window overlooking the busy city.

Daniel knocked on the open door.

"So..."

"Princess..."

Emma laughed.

"I told you."

"I'm not a princess."

Daniel smiled.

"Then who are you?"

Emma looked out at the city lights.

"I'm Emma Carter."

"A girl who finally knows where she came from..."

"But gets to choose where she's going."

She closed the old family photo album and placed it on the shelf.

For the first time in her life...

She wasn't searching for her past.

She was building her future.
